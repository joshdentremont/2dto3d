    Outline

    The area around the logo is filled with this color, out to the smallest oval that contains the whole image (a circle for a square image). It is the same height as that color. Width sets the width of the whole oval.

    The image's corners are rounded off to this radius, cutting away anything in them. Empty areas inside are filled with this color, at the same height as that color.

    Base plate

    Color heights are measured from the top of the base plate.

    Keychain loop

    Frame

    A separate part that follows the shape of your print. Its lip covers the edge of the print from the front, and the print pushes in from the back, face first. Lip depth is measured from the back of the frame, so frame depth minus lip depth is how far the print sits behind the front. If the lip depth leaves room, small bumps behind the print hold it in without glue.

    A pocket in the back of the top border, close to the top edge, for hanging on a nail or screw. It is hidden from the front. Push the head of the nail into the opening, then lower the frame so the lip rests on the shank with the head behind it. Set the diameter of the head, and leave just over 1 mm of the shank showing so the head sits close to the wall.

    A separate foot for standing the frame on a shelf. It plugs into a slot in the back of the bottom border and lies flat on the table behind the frame, which holds the frame leaning back at this angle. The slot is hidden from the front.

    Export

    Multi-color printer

    For AMS, MMU and tool changers. Open the 3MF, or load all the STLs from the ZIP at once and choose “single object with multiple parts”. Then give each part its filament.

    Single extruder

    Turn on Different height per color under Shape to print the colors with one extruder. Each color then shows on top where it is highest, and you change filament at set layers.

    Print the single body and change filament at the layers below.

      In your slicer's layer preview, go to each layer listed and add a filament change (or pause) there. The heights are the top of that layer, as shown on the slicer's layer slider.

      Frame

      Print it as exported, face down, in any color.

      Print the stand as exported, on its side. Then push its short end into the slot in the back of the frame, with the long end flat on the table.
